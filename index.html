Argentina

Argentina spans from subtropical plains in the north to Patagonia's glaciers in the south, with the Andes to the west and the Pampas grasslands at its core. Culturally, it's known for tango, a deep love of football, and cuisine built around beef asado, empanadas, mate, and Malbec wine.
