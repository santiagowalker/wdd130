Choose Your Adventure

From scenic beginner-friendly floats to challenging whitewater, find the river experience that matches your sense of adventure.

Featured Trips

River Explorer

Beginner • 3 Hours • Ages 8+ • $75 per person

A relaxed introduction to rafting with gentle rapids, scenic views, and plenty of time to enjoy the river. This trip is ideal for families, first-time rafters, and anyone looking for a fun but easygoing adventure.

Whitewater Rush

Intermediate • 5 Hours • Ages 12+ • $120 per person

A more energetic trip with faster water, stronger rapids, and exciting technical sections. It’s a good choice for guests who want more action while still having guidance from experienced rafting professionals.

Canyon Challenge

Advanced • Full Day • Ages 16+ • $185 per person

A full-day adventure through challenging rapids, narrow canyon sections, and dramatic river scenery. This trip is designed for confident rafters who want a more intense and physically demanding experience.
