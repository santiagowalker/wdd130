Favorite City

Buenos Aires is the vibrant, bustling capital of Argentina, located on the western shore of the Río de la Plata. Often called the "Paris of South America," the city blends European-style neoclassical architecture and wide boulevards with a passionate, distinct Latin American energy. It is famous for its rich cultural life, historic tango traditions, world-class theaters, and lively neighborhoods like San Telmo and Palermo.
